WHAT THIS IS
Published aggregates from 8,494 list prices for pergola kits sold to US buyers,
from 73 companies, captured 24-27 September 2026. Kit prices in USD before tax,
shipping, footings and installation. No individual product listings are included.

FILE
pergola-price-index-2026-q4.csv
Line 1 is the header row. Values are USD per square foot of nominal covered
area unless the table says USD (typical price at size).
One long-format table; the "table" column says which summary a row belongs to.

TABLES (values of the "table" column)
- category $/sq ft: median, quartiles, min and max $/sq ft by roof category
- $/sq ft by size band: median $/sq ft within size bands (e.g. <=100 sq ft, 101-150)
- typical price at size (USD): median and quartile kit price at common sizes (10x10, 12x12 ...)
- family $/sq ft (category too small for a median): product-family figures shown instead
- marketplace benchmark vs brand-direct, size-matched ($/sq ft)

COLUMNS
table         which summary table the row belongs to (see above)
row           roof category, or category plus product family
size_or_band  size (e.g. 10x10) or size band; blank for category-level rows
median        median value ($/sq ft, or USD for "typical price at size")
p25           25th percentile
p75           75th percentile
min           minimum observed value
max           maximum observed value
companies     number of companies behind the row
families      number of product families behind the row
price_points  number of individual prices behind the row
label         notes: limited sample, reason a median is not published, or benchmark gap

METHOD (summary)
Categories are set by roof type first, frame second. Prices are for a complete,
usable kit: a required component with a public price is added; a kit whose
required component has no public price is excluded. Also excluded: bracket-only
and vinyl-sleeve kits, accessories, bundles, quote-only products, items out of
stock for more than 90 days, soft-top gazebos, arbors, and products named as
carports, sunrooms, sheds, awnings or patio covers. A median is published only
with at least 3 product families from 3 companies.
